## 3. Full Description

**Take complete control of your personal finances with total privacy.**

SilentSums is an offline-first money and expense manager built on a simple premise: your financial life is nobody's business but yours. With zero cloud dependencies, no account logins, and no background analytics, SilentSums keeps every transaction, account balance, and financial receipt strictly on your device.

### Key Features

* **Absolute Privacy**: No accounts, no registration, and no cloud logins required.
* **100% Local Storage**: Your financial ledger stays entirely inside your phone's encrypted private storage.
* **Open CSV Portability**: Own your data unconditionally. Export and import complete ledger backups in universal CSV format anytime with zero proprietary lock-in.
* **Automated Rolling Backups**: Automatically backs up daily snapshots to your device's Downloads or custom Storage Access Framework (SAF) folder without sending a single byte over the wire.
* **Split Transactions**: Accurately split bills across multiple categories, tracking multi-category purchases and group outings with mathematical precision.
* **Rich Attachments (Photos & Voice Notes)**: Capture receipt photos and dictate voice memos directly alongside transactions, safely housed in a private on-device media vault.
* **Smart Dues & Debt Tracker**: Keep clear tabs on who owes you and who you owe with partial settlement support, upcoming due reminders, and balance rollups.
* **Built-in Smart Calculator**: Calculate arithmetic expressions directly in number fields with automatic operator evaluation.
* **Custom Categories & Accounts**: Track cash, bank accounts, credit cards, and digital wallets with personalized icons and colors.
* **Privacy Mode & App Lock**: Mask monetary numbers with one tap in public, and secure the app behind biometric fingerprint or PIN lock.

## 5. Plain-Language Privacy Policy Summary

* **No Internet Permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`)**: SilentSums does not even declare the Android internet permission in its manifest. The application cannot connect to the internet, network servers, or third-party cloud backends under any circumstances.
* **Zero Telemetry or Analytics**: There are no tracking SDKs, no ad networks, no crash collection services, and no analytics libraries embedded in the application.
* **Data Stays On Device**: All ledger records, accounts, categories, receipt images, voice recordings, and audit logs reside strictly on your device's local storage.
* **User-Controlled Export**: You have full control over when and where your data is exported. Backups are written as plain CSV files that you can move, copy, or delete at your discretion.
* **Crash Log Privacy**: If the app crashes, you can copy or share the technical crash log yourself; it is never sent automatically and contains no financial data.
