What's actually in the box

Everything a money app needs. Nothing that phones home.

Built for real life — the ₹170 tea on the highway home, the car EMI due on the 10th — without a single byte leaving your device.

Truly offline

The INTERNET permission is absent from the manifest — the app is technically incapable of phoning home. Your ledger lives in a local Room (SQLite) database in private app storage.

Dues & recurring engine

Subscriptions, EMIs, loans, IOUs — with optional due reminders. "Record & mark paid" writes the ledger entry and advances the cycle; skip-cycle covers the month you don't pay.

Multi-source net worth

Cash, bank accounts, and credit cards tracked as separate sources, with net worth that includes card debt. Budget limits show green below 80%, amber from 80%, and red at 100%.

Privacy mode & app lock

One tap turns every number into •••••• — no shoulder-surfing on the bus. Behind it: optional biometric unlock and a salted PBKDF2 PIN. While privacy mode or app lock is on, FLAG_SECURE blocks screenshots and Recent-Apps previews.

Rich attachments

Attach receipt photos and record voice notes on a transaction. They live in a private on-device media vault — backup files don't carry them, so an optional Mirror setting copies them to a folder you choose, to survive a reinstall or a new phone.

CSV & JSON you own

Export your ledger — transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, recurring commitments and custom fields — to a human-readable CSV for any spreadsheet. A JSON snapshot of accounts, categories and transactions is available too. Import either back anytime. No proprietary lock-in.

Defense in depth

Financial data deserves real engineering

Not a privacy pinky-promise — specific, verifiable protections in the source.

Salted PBKDF2 PIN hashingPINs are never stored in plaintext: PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256, 120,000 iterations, 16-byte random salt, constant-time verification.
Brute-force lockoutFrom 5 failed attempts, exponential backoff locks the keypad (30s up to 15 min) — tracked across reboots, while biometric unlock stays available.
Screenshot protectionFLAG_SECURE is applied whenever the lock screen or Privacy Mode is active — no screenshots, recordings, or Recent-Apps thumbnails of your balances.
Cloud backup disabledallowBackup="false" plus explicit exclusions for every data domain — your ledger is never silently uploaded by Android's backup service.
PIN scrubbing on exportBackups never include your PIN hash or lockout state, and restores ignore any PIN or lock fields in a file — a backup can't carry or overwrite your lock.
Narrow FileProvider scopeOnly two dedicated cache folders are ever shareable — never the database or root storage.

Privacy, plainly

Your balances never leave your phone — the files you choose to export are the only thing that does.

No network permissionSilentSums can't reach the internet even if it wanted to — the permission isn't in the app.
No analytics, no adsNothing is measured, tracked, or sold. There's no SDK for it to go through.
Storage stays localYour ledger, media vault, and settings live in private on-device storage, excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.
